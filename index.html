Queue soldiers in the Build bar (click = add, right-click = remove) — each costs XP, time & population.
Select soldiers and click a node to claim it, or an enemy to attack. Move them to empty ground to station a guard post.
Idle soldiers hold their post & auto-defend — press G to rally all free units. Spend level-up buff points on the left.
Cut an enemy's trunk link and their branch goes dark. Destroy their Base to win.
