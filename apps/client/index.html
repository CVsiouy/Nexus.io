Your base mines gold

It earns on its own, forever. Spend it on soldiers, on walls to ring your base, or on mining upgrades to earn faster.

Fight in squads of 15

New soldiers wait safely inside your base. A squad must reach a full 15 before it can move out — the left panel shows READY when it can.

Attacking is a commitment

A squad sent to attack cannot be recalled until its target falls. Walls block attackers except where they are broken, so defenders behind a wall have a real edge.

Two bosses hold the centre

Walled, guarded, and worth +2 gold/sec forever to whoever lands the kill. They never heal or rebuild, so damage sticks — and in team mode you can click an ally's base to donate a soldier.
